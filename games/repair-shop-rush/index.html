How Rewards Work

Shop Balance starts at $300 once, then persists across shifts and sessions. Service payments and tips add to it; parts, paid tests, Tech Vision, and upgrades spend it.

Reputation starts at 50. Good, efficient work builds customer confidence (0–100).

Score measures successful, efficient troubleshooting. It is not currency.

Friday Arcade XP is persistent progression, awarded deterministically after every shift.

Arcade Tokens are persistent currency: +1 for 3/4 repairs, +1 for all 4, and +1 for an efficient clean sweep. Standard tokens are available on the first completed shift each local day.
